# Getting Started

Welcome! This guide walks you through the key features of Wiki AI.

## Navigating the Wiki

Use the **sidebar** on the left to browse topics by category. Each section expands to show individual pages.

You can also use the **search bar** at the top (`⌘K`) to find pages by title or content.

## Using AI Chat

Click **Ask AI** in the header to open the AI chat interface. You can ask questions like:

- "What is retrieval-augmented generation?"
- "How do I write a good system prompt?"
- "Explain the difference between fine-tuning and RAG"

The AI searches the entire wiki and returns answers with **source citations** linking back to the relevant pages.

## Wiki Links

Pages reference each other using `[[wiki links]]`. When you see a highlighted link, click it to navigate to that page.
