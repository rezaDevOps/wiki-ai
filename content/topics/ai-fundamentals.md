# AI Fundamentals

Artificial intelligence is the field of building systems that can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence.

## Key Concepts

### Machine Learning

Machine learning is a subset of AI where systems learn patterns from data rather than following explicit rules.

**Types of learning:**
- **Supervised** — learns from labeled examples
- **Unsupervised** — finds patterns in unlabeled data
- **Reinforcement** — learns through trial and feedback

### Large Language Models (LLMs)

LLMs are neural networks trained on massive text corpora. They predict the next token in a sequence, and through this simple objective, learn rich representations of language, code, and reasoning.

Key models: GPT-4, Claude 3, Gemini, Llama 3.

### Embeddings

Embeddings are dense vector representations of text. Similar texts have similar embeddings (high cosine similarity). This is the foundation of semantic search and RAG.
